GOOGLE MAPS KHONG CAN KEY - CHI SUA FRONTEND

Ban nay duoc sua tu file takivivu (11) (1).zip ban gui.

- Ban do khach san va diem tham quan dung khung Google Maps khong can API key.
- Nhap dia chi trong bieu mau de cap nhat ban do; van giu cac o chon dia chi cu.
- Co lien ket Mo Google Maps va Chi duong.
- Khung nhung khong tu dien dia chi/toa do khi bam vao ban do. Dia chi duoc luu
  theo bieu mau; toa do da co duoc dung khi chua thay doi dia chi.
- Frontend khong goi Map Service de tim dia chi, lay toa do hoac tim dia diem.
- Khong can cau hinh key trinh duyet/backend cho phan ban do nay.

Ngoai frontend, chi doi spring.datasource.password=123456 theo yeu cau cua ban
trong 8 service co database (ca resources va ban sao target/classes).
Tat ca file backend con lai, database, Maven va cau hinh IntelliJ .idea/Run
duoc giu nguyen tu ZIP goc. Khong can bo sung cau hinh Run moi, khong can import
lai database va khong can chay SQL. Cau hinh nay dung mat khau MySQL 123456;
khong tu thay doi mat khau tai khoan MySQL dang cai tren may.

Chay backend nhu truoc. Trong thu muc frontend, chay npm run dev nhu cu.
Neu chua co thu vien hoac thu vien loi: npm ci, sau do npm run dev.

Kiem tra logic ban do: node --test tests/googleMaps.test.cjs
Kiem tra TypeScript: npx tsc -b

Khung Google Maps can ket noi Internet va co the bi chan boi trinh duyet/Google.
Neu khung khong tai duoc, dung nut Mo Google Maps. Chua xac minh khung nhung
tren trinh duyet thuc te cua ban; cac kiem tra tu dong chi kiem tra code va URL.
